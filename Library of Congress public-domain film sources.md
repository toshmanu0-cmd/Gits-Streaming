# Library of Congress public-domain film sources

## Rights guidance

Source: https://www.loc.gov/collections/national-screening-room/about-this-collection/rights-and-access/

The Library of Congress states that it is not aware of U.S. copyright or other restrictions in the vast majority of motion pictures in the National Screening Room and that, absent such restrictions, the materials are free to use and reuse. It also states that rights assessment remains the user’s responsibility and that some items may have copyright, privacy, publicity, licensing, trademark, or foreign-country restrictions. Each item should be checked individually.

## The Great Train Robbery (1903)

Source and playback page: https://www.loc.gov/item/00694220/

The item page has a built-in Video Player and direct MP4 download. It identifies the film as a 1903 Edison Manufacturing Co. production, a National Film Registry selection, and an 11:54 digital viewing copy. The item page is the stable source link for playback and attribution.

## The Flying Ace (1926)

Source and playback page: https://www.loc.gov/item/2021604060

The item page has a built-in Video Player and direct MP4 download. It identifies the film as originally released in 1926 by Norman Film Manufacturing Company, a National Film Registry selection, and a 66-minute digital video. The page’s rights notice repeats the Library’s item-level rights caveat, so the site should link visitors to the source page rather than mirror the file.

## Watch-list context

Source: https://www.loc.gov/programs/national-film-preservation-board/film-registry/watch-films-on-the-national-film-registry/

The Library says it cannot post all National Film Registry selections because of copyright concerns, but provides some films in the National Screening Room, some in the public domain, and other approved viewing links. Therefore the catalog uses direct Library of Congress item pages and visible attribution.
